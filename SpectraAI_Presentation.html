End-to-End Workflow

The 6-Stage Deterministic Pipeline

Executing sub-second attribute extraction, fusion, taxonomy mapping, and validation.

1. Multimodal Ingestion

Ingests PDF, nameplate photos, and CSV rows. Computes cryptographic SHA-256 hashes on all raw sources.

2. Vision VLM Extraction

Extracts technical attributes via Claude 3.5 Sonnet / OCR. Captures exact page coordinates, bounding boxes, and raw snippets.

3. Concordance & Fusion

Cross-references sources. Agreement boosts confidence to 1.0; conflicting values trigger a 0.7x penalty for human review.

4. RAG Taxonomy Enrichment

Standardizes under UNSPSC v24.0 and ETIM 9.0. Fills default warranty and standard certifications from Seed KB.

5. Knowledge Graph Engine

Expands NetworkX graph topology. Runs Z-Score outlier checks and computes equivalent substitute replacements.

6. CRI Validation & Review

Calculates 0–100 Commerce Readiness Score. Routes low-confidence or conflicted fields to the Human Review portal.
